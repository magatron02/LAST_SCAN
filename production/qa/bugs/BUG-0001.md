## Reproduction Steps
**Preconditions**: Start the prototype, click the overlay to acquire PointerLock, and remain in NAVIGATE mode.

1. Hold `W` so the player is moving.
2. Cause one animation frame to stall for more than 0.1 seconds while PointerLock remains active, for example with a debugger pause or a heavy main-thread stall.
3. Resume execution while `W` is still held.
4. Observe the player position during the first resumed frame.

**Expected Result**: Movement uses `dt = min(frameDelta, 0.1)` and advances at most `MOVE_SPEED * 0.1 = 0.16m` in one frame, as required by FPS Movement AC-EC03.

**Actual Result**: The complete uncapped value returned by `clock.getDelta()` is multiplied by `MOVE_SPEED`. In the current rectangular prototype the final position is clamped to the room bounds, but the player can jump a large distance or directly to a boundary in one frame.

## Notes
Recommended implementation direction: extract a testable `updateMovement(dt)` function and cap its input with `Math.min(dt, 0.1)`. Add a unit test that injects `dt = 0.5` and verifies the applied displacement is no more than 0.16m. Do not rely on the final room-bound clamp as the only protection; future internal walls and notched-room blockers may be crossed before their collision checks run.
